行走克羅地亞 見山還是山
Walking Croatia: Back to the Essence

2025 · 09 · 27  ·  遊記

初遇克羅地亞，見山是山，無處不是歷史傷疤。一面面牆壁、一座座雕像、一幢幢荒廢建築，時刻低訴那些年的動盪。

A land layered with scars of history. The first encounter with Croatia reveals what seemed simple. Walls, statues, abandoned buildings, each murmuring of past turmoil.

巴爾幹半島風雲，血淚交織，深入基因，當地人人知曉；朋友聚頭，如無必要，最好不提，幾乎自動波了。然而其他一切大小事，隨便找個路人搭咀，卻能聊個天南地北。

The storms of the Balkans, stitched with blood and tears, lie deep in memory. Among friends it is rarely spoken, silence falling by instinct. Yet on every other matter, even with strangers, conversation drifts easily from one shore to another.

歷史和溫情，光影鮮明。幾個晨昏，是暖還是冷？見山不是山。

History and warmth, light and shadow, a vivid contrast. After a few dawns and dusks, warmth and cold blur, certainty fades, and what once seemed clear no longer is.

咖啡一杯接一杯，談笑不休，千島之國日常。講回憶、講成長、講家庭、講現在、講社會、講將來；會大笑、會感動、會慨嘆、會憤怒、會無奈。百感交雜皆因愛。

Cup after cup of coffee, conversation spills without pause. The rhythm of daily life in the land of a thousand islands. Memories, childhood, family, the present, society, the future; laughter, tenderness, lament, anger, helplessness: a chorus of feelings, all rooted in love.

上代活下來，這代活下去。傳承的是溫度，談笑間總有一份堅毅。行走克羅地亞，平凡之處盡非凡。這片土地最動人之處，不只在山河壯麗，更在於真實。

The last generation endured, the next continues. What is handed down is warmth, and within laughter rests a quiet resilience. In Croatia, the ordinary is never ordinary. What moves the heart lies not in the landscape’s grandeur alone, but in its truth.

但願人長久，見山還是山。

May endurance remain. And in the end, all returns to the beginning. Familiar, yet seen as if for the first time.
