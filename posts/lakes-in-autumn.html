憶湖區秋深
A Return to the Lakes in Autumn

2025 · 12 · 02  ·  遊記

歲晚寒流撲來，最宜窩藏家中。蓋上暖毯，手捧熱茶，呆望窗前。年度最好回憶，漸次浮現。

As the winter chill sweeps in, there's nothing better than staying cocooned at home, wrapped in a blanket, with a cup of hot tea in hand, gazing idly through the window. The year's fondest memories begin to resurface.

我於是想起十月那趟公路之旅。其時英格蘭鄉間美學達到最高峰，湖區靜謐山水之間，溫和秋日之下，芬草翠綠之上，添染層層黃褐與焦紅。一步一畫，幕幕優雅。

I am drawn back to that road trip in October, when the English countryside was at its most beautiful. The Lake District was calm and radiant under a soft autumn sun; green fields turned shades of gold and rust, every turn revealing another perfect view.

湖區之美，在於地貌緊湊多變。在科尼斯頓（Coniston）租住民宿，以此為中心探索坎布里亞南峽谷，半小時車程內可遊遍多個經典小鎮與湖泊。走入霍克斯黑（Hawkshead）之古樸村落，聽窄巷和石屋低訴碧雅翠絲（Beatrix Potter）的童話。格拉斯米爾（Grasmere）山環水抱，有份難以言喻之祥和，無怪孕育出華茲華斯（William Wordsworth）此派浪漫詩人。安布賽德（Ambleside）則如一個熱鬧驛站，是每個戶外活動愛好者之完美補給站。個個村鎮各具特色，人與自然和諧共存，日子樸實而堅毅。

The charm of the Lakes lies in its compact and varied landscape. From a stay in Coniston, many of the region's most beloved towns lie within half an hour's reach. In the quaint village of Hawkshead, narrow lanes and stone cottages whispered Beatrix Potter's tales. Grasmere, encircled by hills, carried a peace beyond words, and little wonder that Wordsworth's romantic verses were born there. Ambleside, lively and warm, felt like a travellers' haven, a place for every outdoor soul to pause and replenish. Each village stood distinct, yet together they formed a portrait of simple days, steady hearts, and a harmony between people and nature.

最難忘一日，莫過於登上英格蘭之巔斯科費爾峰（Scafell Pike）。三十多歲阿叔如我，腰痠背痛已是日常，再無少年行山遠足那份勇；加上當日狂風大作，山徑寸步難行，步步攀升都在考驗毅力。幸有旅伴鼓勵，在一塊塊鬆動碎石走過六小時，最終安全返回旅舍。那份極致疲累而來之滿足感，比眼前萬千風景更為深刻。

The most unforgettable day was the climb up Scafell Pike, the highest peak in England. For a man in his thirties, with a sore back and fading stamina, hiking no longer feels like the carefree adventure it once was. With gales howling across the ridge, each upward step became a test of sheer willpower. Yet with my companion's encouragement, we pushed on over loose rock for six gruelling hours before we finally made it back to the lodge in one piece. The satisfaction that followed exhaustion was deeper than any view.

遠離喧囂日常，腦袋輕鬆不少。晨間露水，小船倒影，還有賀德威克羊（Herdwick）吃草的畫面，我都清晰記下來。寒風凜冽十二月，坐在室內，回想那趟旅程。那份濃厚秋意，恍惚仍在眼前。伴隨而來的，是從容，是力量，也讓我對來年旅程多了一份期待。

Stepping away from the daily bustle, my mind was noticeably lighter. I clearly recall the morning dew, the reflections of the boats, and the hardy Herdwick sheep grazing peacefully. As December's chill sets in and I sit indoors reflecting on the trip, that deep autumnal mood vividly returns. What lingered was a quiet composure, a renewed strength, and a gentle anticipation for the journeys to come.
